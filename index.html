The extension requests permission for the active site and asks you to approve sending its cookies. Set a strong APP_PASSWORD on Render first.

How do I get a cookies.txt file?
  1. Sign in to the site (for example instagram.com) in your normal browser.
  2. Install a cookies exporter extension, such as "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY".
  3. Open the site, click the extension and choose Export.
  4. Come back here and choose the saved file. Some exports contain cookies for many sites. Check the domains shown, treat the file like a password, and delete it from your computer after use.

Uploaded cookies are kept in server memory, not saved as a cookie file. Closing this tab, signing out, or removing cookies requests cleanup and cancels its active work.
